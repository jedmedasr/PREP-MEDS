# Apixaban — clinician-review scenario adjudication matrix (2026-11-08)

**Status: IN_PROGRESS — research-only.** Companion to `docs/antithrombotic-source-reconciliation.md`; not an order set, patient-facing hold schedule, or clinical approval. Previous evidence covers ASGE 2016 Table 7, BSG/ESGE 2021, ACG/CAG 2022, CHEST 2022, ACC/AHA 2023/2024, IDEN 2024, and FDA April 2025. This document adds a *scenario-specific, source-discordance matrix* without duplicating the prior guideline narrative. Current FDA revision remains unverified.

## Primary-source pointers and scope
- **ASGE 2016**, *Gastrointest Endosc* 83:3–16, DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gie.2015.09.035, Tables 2–3 and **7**: historical low-risk DOAC continuation; high-risk apixaban Table 7 CrCl >60 = 1–2 days, 30–59 = 3 days, 15–29 = 4 days. No explicit exactly-60 or <15 row; no pediatric formulation/tube-specific interruption. Table 7 alone does not establish restart, bridging, or dual/triple-therapy plans.
- **ACG/CAG 2022**, elective Statement 13, https://gi.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/ACG-CAG-Guideline.pdf: temporary DOAC interruption suggested (conditional, very-low certainty); no apixaban-specific universally applicable duration.
- **BSG/ESGE 2021**, DOI https://doi.org/10.1136/gutjnl-2021-325184, DOAC and post-elective sections: low-risk omit morning dose (weak, low quality); high-risk last dose three days before (strong, low quality); resumption up to 2–3 days individualized (strong, moderate quality). Five-day renal exception is **dabigatran**, not apixaban.
- **IDEN 2024**, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11391130/, Statements 11–14: low-risk omit morning dose (conditional, very low); high-risk withhold **more than 48 h** (strong, low); high-risk resume within 2–3 days *once hemostasis secured* (conditional, very low).
- **CHEST 2022**, DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chest.2022.07.025, Recommendation 22: apixaban 1–2-day preprocedure interruption (conditional, very-low certainty); perioperative rather than endoscopy-specific. No routine DOAC heparin bridging.
- **ACC/AHA/ACCP/HRS 2023 AF**, DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacc.2023.08.017, Table 18: AF with CrCl **>25**, one or two *full* days without dosing before low/high risk, plus no dose procedure day. Do not apply AF table to pediatric VTE or exactly 25.
- **FDA ELIQUIS April 2025**, https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/220073s000lbl.pdf, §2.4: at least 24 h for low-risk and at least 48 h for moderate/high-risk invasive procedures, restart after adequate hemostasis; label minimum is not the IDEN **strictly greater than 48 h** recommendation. Label currentness requires recheck.

## Distinct scenarios that must remain visible to clinician

| Scenario / source boundary | Research comparison and disagreement | Required clinician review / fail-closed assertion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diagnostic EGD ± biopsy, adult oral BID | Historical ASGE 2016 favors continuation; BSG/ESGE 2021 and IDEN 2024 omit morning dose; ACG/CAG 2022 suggests temporary interruption without fixed days; CHEST 2022 general-procedure comparator differs. | Do not emit one consensus instruction. Document procedure risk, indication, dose schedule, local endoscopy policy. |
| Diagnostic colonoscopy ± biopsy, adult oral BID | Same low-risk discordance; anticipated polypectomy may change classification. | Require explicit therapeutic-intervention expectation; do not infer diagnostic-only risk from procedure label. |
| Combined EGD + colonoscopy with possible polypectomy | Endoscopy risk depends on planned/anticipated intervention, not number of procedures. | Unknown therapeutic risk => review required, no automatically selected interval. |
| Planned high-risk polypectomy/EMR/ESD/sphincterotomy | ASGE 2016 CrCl-specific 1–4 days versus BSG/ESGE 2021 last dose 3 days prior; IDEN 2024 **>48 h** versus FDA **>=48 h**; CHEST 2022 2 days for high risk and AF 2023 full-days rule differ in time semantics. | Preserve all source-specific alternatives, last-dose clock time and procedure clock time; do not choose a universal interval. |
| CrCl exactly **60.0 mL/min** | ASGE Table 7 has >60 and 30–59, no explicit exactly-60 category. | `UNASSIGNED_ASGE_BOUNDARY`; do not round to 59 or 60.1 or auto-prescribe. |
| CrCl **59.5**, **29.5**, **<15 mL/min** | ASGE printed integer-bracket categories do not explicitly resolve fractional gaps 59–60, 29–30; <15 outside Table 7. | `UNASSIGNED_ASGE_BOUNDARY` unless primary text explicitly resolves; renal instability also escalates. |
| CrCl **25.0 mL/min**, AF | ACC 2023 Table 18 applies only to **>25**; ASGE 2016 has separate high-risk category 15–29. | Do not copy ACC table at exactly 25 or invent added-day interval. |
| Recent VTE, stroke, high thrombotic risk, or dual/triple antithrombotics | General DOAC hold schedules do not resolve urgent thrombosis/stent risk or concomitant antiplatelet changes. | Individualized prescribing/cardiology/hematology discussion; bridging not inferred from older warfarin language. |
| Child-Pugh B/C, strong CYP3A4/P-gp inhibitor/inducer, unstable renal function | FDA label §8.7/§7/§2.6 supplies prescribing cautions, not validated alternate endoscopy hold durations. | Flag interactions and hepatic disease; no automatic arithmetic extension. |
| Crushed oral/NG administration, pediatric sprinkle capsule or oral suspension | FDA has formulation/administration instructions; ASGE 2016 **NOT ADDRESSED** for pediatric/tube-specific interruption. | Adult timing must not inherit to pediatric dosing; adjudicate age, weight, indication, route and feeding tube separately. |
| Post-polypectomy/EMR/ESD delayed bleeding or uncertain hemostasis | IDEN high-risk restart 2–3 days conditional on adequate hemostasis; BSG/ESGE restart individualized; FDA restart after adequate hemostasis. | No unconditional restart clock or default bridging. |

## Canonical inheritance and acceptance

The existing canonical record `MED-apixaban` is **formulation unspecified**. Treat mapping to `ING-apixaban` as provisional, **not** proof that adult oral, pediatric, crushed/NG, and suspension regimens are clinically interchangeable. For every future brand/strength/formulation/combination canonical ID, preserve its immutable ID, ingredient membership, distinct delivery group, and explicit ASGE 2016 comparison or `NOT_ADDRESSED` determination. Reject cross-inheritance from rivaroxaban, dabigatran, and combination products.

**Acceptance tests still required (not executed by this document):** exact >48 versus >=48 hours; BID dosing and clock-time examples; CrCl 60/59.5/59/30/29.5/29/25/<15; low/unknown/high-risk EGD/colonoscopy/combined; adult vs pediatric vs tube; interacting medications, liver disease, high thrombosis, restart after confirmed hemostasis, and negative cross-ingredient mapping. Evidence revision/hash and clinician primary-source verification occur at authenticated Mark Reviewed; do not mark `TESTED` or `CLINICALLY_REVIEWED` until requirements are satisfied.
