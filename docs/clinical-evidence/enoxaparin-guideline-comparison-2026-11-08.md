# Enoxaparin (LMWH) — primary-guideline periendoscopic comparison, 2026-11-08

**Bucket:** 01 (Lane A). **Group:** `ING-enoxaparin`. **Status:** IN_PROGRESS; research-only, not patient-facing instructions or clinician approval. This comparison distinguishes *therapeutic LMWH used to bridge interrupted warfarin* from *chronic therapeutic LMWH for VTE* and *prophylactic LMWH*. A bridge protocol cannot automatically become a universal hold instruction for all three.

## Direct primary sources and exact sections
1. **ASGE Standards of Practice Committee, 2016**, *Management of antithrombotic agents for patients undergoing GI endoscopy*, *Gastrointest Endosc* 83(1):3–16, DOI [10.1016/j.gie.2015.09.035](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gie.2015.09.035), [official full text](https://www.asge.org/home/resources/publications/guidelines/2016_antithrombotics), **Table 2**, *Parenteral and subcutaneously administered anticoagulants*, **Table 3** (procedure bleeding risk), **Table 13** (elective management). Table 2 gives LMWH (enoxaparin/dalteparin) duration of action **24 hours**; narrative specifies last therapeutic bridging dose **24 hours before the anticipated procedure at 50% of total daily dose**. Table 13 suggests bridging selected high-thrombotic-risk patients undergoing high-risk procedures (historical recommendation, ⊕⊕○○). It does not furnish a single validated interval for every prophylactic-dose, renal-impaired, or pediatric LMWH scenario.
2. **BSG/ESGE, Veitch et al., 2021**, *Gut* 70:1611–1628, DOI [10.1136/gutjnl-2021-325184](https://doi.org/10.1136/gutjnl-2021-325184), [official PDF](https://www.bsg.org.uk/BSG/media/BSG-Media/BSG_ESGE-antiplatelet-and-anticoagulant-update-2021.pdf), **Anticoagulants → Warfarin and heparin; Figure 2; Table 3**. For **high-risk endoscopy plus high thrombotic risk requiring warfarin bridging**, stop warfarin 5 days prior, begin therapeutic LMWH 2 days after warfarin cessation, administer final LMWH **at least 24 hours before** endoscopy, check INR <1.5, resume warfarin evening of procedure and LMWH next day until INR therapeutic. The bridging recommendation is **strong**, with low-to-moderate evidence descriptions in the document. These are conditional *warfarin-bridge* steps, not a universal enoxaparin monotherapy schedule.
3. **ACG/CAG 2022**, [guideline PDF](https://gi.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/ACG-CAG-Guideline.pdf), elective endoscopy statements concerning warfarin bridging: generally **suggest against routine bridging** when warfarin is held, except selected mechanical-valve patients; conditional, low-certainty evidence. Thus older ASGE/BSG bridge pathways and ACG/CAG's avoidance of routine bridging must be presented as distinct, indication-dependent options rather than combined into a single rule.

## Clinical scenario adjudication
| Scenario | Guideline comparison | Clinical gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diagnostic EGD/colonoscopy with or without biopsy | ASGE Table 3 classifies diagnostic endoscopy/biopsy low bleeding risk; BSG/ESGE Figure 2 likewise | Do not apply high-risk bridging hold to low-risk diagnostic procedures without indication-specific review |
| Polypectomy, EMR/ESD, sphincterotomy, EUS sampling | ASGE Table 3 and BSG/ESGE Table 1 classify higher bleeding risk | Confirm actual planned intervention, not merely procedure name |
| High-risk endoscopy, high-thrombotic-risk warfarin patient | ASGE 2016 and BSG/ESGE 2021 provide LMWH bridging pathways with final LMWH ≥24h prior; ACG/CAG 2022 discourages *routine* bridging, with exceptions | Require thrombotic indication, mechanical valve/recent VTE assessment, prescriber decision |
| Therapeutic enoxaparin for active VTE, not bridging | Warfarin bridge instructions do **not** establish all therapeutic LMWH interruption/restart cases | Distinct group/scenario assessment; no automatic inheritance |
| Prophylactic enoxaparin | Therapeutic bridging instructions cannot safely be transferred to prophylactic dosing | Separate regimen and renal-function assessment |
| Renal impairment, obesity/low weight, pregnancy, pediatric use | These primary endoscopy passages do not supply universal renal-specific or weight-specific interruption schedules | Explicitly unresolved; require individual clinician judgment |
| Post-procedure bleeding or insecure hemostasis | Post-procedure LMWH bridge restart from BSG/ESGE assumes appropriate clinical conditions | No automatic resumption without adequate hemostasis and thrombotic/bleeding review |
| Combined EGD + colonoscopy | Use the highest planned intervention bleeding risk, not the low-risk diagnostic label alone | Escalate if possible polypectomy/therapy not yet established |
