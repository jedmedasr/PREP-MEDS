ASGE 2016 is retained as historical comparator, not automatically the preferred current pathway.

## Direct primary-source evidence
- **ASGE Standards of Practice Committee, 2016**, *Management of antithrombotic agents for patients undergoing GI endoscopy*, *Gastrointest Endosc* 83:3–16, [official guideline and Table 6](https://www.asge.org/home/resources/publications/guidelines/2016_antithrombotics), DOI [10.1016/j.gie.2015.09.035](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gie.2015.09.035). **Table 6**, dabigatran, *moderate procedural bleeding risk / high procedural bleeding risk* interruption by CrCl: **>80:** 1–1.5 / 2–3 days; **50–80:** 1–2 / 2–3 days; **30–49:** 1.5–2 / 3–4 days; **≤29:** 2–3 / 4–6 days. These historical ranges are not an instruction to use dabigatran in severe renal disease. Table 6 does not supply an exact interpretation for fractional CrCl gaps (49–50, 80–80+), and does not supply pediatric or formulation-specific endoscopic intervals. The guideline distinguishes low-risk diagnostic endoscopy from high-risk therapeutic procedures in its procedure-risk table.
- **BSG/ESGE, Veitch et al., 2021**, *Gut* 70:1611–1628, [direct DOAC section](https://gut.bmj.com/content/70/9/1611), DOI [10.1136/gutjnl-2021-325184](https://doi.org/10.1136/gutjnl-2021-325184). Low-risk endoscopy: **omit morning DOAC dose** (weak, low-quality). High-risk: last DOAC dose **3 days before** (strong, low-quality), **dabigatran with CrCl/eGFR 30–50: 5 days before** (strong, low-quality); deteriorating renal function: hematology consultation. Postprocedure: restart up to **2–3 days**, individualized to hemorrhagic and thrombotic risk (strong, moderate-quality). **Important source discrepancy:** the guideline's headline recommendation explicitly says 5 days for 30–50, whereas a narrative passage in one published version says 3–5 days; present the explicit recommendation and flag textual discordance rather than silently replacing it.
- **ACG/CAG, Abraham et al., 2022**, *Am J Gastroenterol* 117:542–558, [guideline](https://gi.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/ACG-CAG-Guideline.pdf), DOI [10.14309/ajg.0000000000001627](https://doi.org/10.14309/ajg.0000000000001627), **elective endoscopy Statement 13**: suggest temporary DOAC interruption over continuation (conditional, very-low certainty); **no dabigatran-specific renal hold table**. Insufficient evidence for same-day versus 1–7-day resumption.
- **CHEST, Douketis et al., 2022**, *Chest* 162:e207–e243, [Recommendation 23 and implementation considerations](https://journal.chestnet.org/article/S0012-3692(22)01364-2/abstract), DOI [10.1016/j.chest.2022.07.025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chest.2022.07.025). Suggest interruption **1–4 days** (conditional, very-low certainty). Low/moderate procedural risk: **1 day off CrCl ≥50**, **2 days off CrCl <50**; high risk: **2 days off CrCl ≥50**, **4 days off CrCl <50**. Perioperative guidance, not endoscopy-specific; this creates a clinically meaningful discrepancy with BSG/ESGE 5 days for CrCl 30–50 high-risk endoscopy.
- **IDEN consensus 2024**, [Statements 11–14 and Figure 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11391130/): low-risk omit morning dose (conditional, very low); high-risk **withhold >48 h** (strong, low), but **dabigatran with CrCl <50**: last dose **5 days before** high-risk procedure (Figure 3); resume 2–3 days after high-risk procedure **only after adequate hemostasis** (conditional, very low). At CrCl exactly 50, do not apply the strictly <50 exception without checking the applicable selected guideline.
- **ACC/AHA AF-specific perioperative guidance:** evaluate only if AF indication and patient eligibility apply; do not transfer AF recommendations to pediatric VTE or imply that the AF table supersedes endoscopy-specific guidance. Exact applicable table passage requires separate extraction before marking this source reconciled.

## Scenarios and non-interchangeable distinctions
| Scenario | Research finding / clinician review question |
| --- | --- |
| Diagnostic EGD/colonoscopy ± biopsy | BSG/ESGE and IDEN omit morning DOAC dose; CHEST 1 day off at CrCl ≥50 or 2 at <50 for low/moderate risk; ACG/CAG suggests temporary interruption without drug-specific days. Distinct guideline pathways, not one universal instruction. |
| High-risk polypectomy, EMR/ESD, sphincterotomy, EUS sampling | Compare BSG/ESGE last dose 3 days before versus IDEN >48h and CHEST 2 days off at CrCl ≥50; for CrCl 30–49 compare BSG/ESGE and IDEN 5-day versus CHEST 4-day guidance. |
| CrCl exactly 50 | BSG/ESGE headline includes 50 in 30–50 five-day band; IDEN Figure 3 uses <50, CHEST uses <50; **discordance at exactly 50 must remain visible**. |
| CrCl 29 or below / rapidly deteriorating | ASGE historical ≤29 row is not authorization for treatment; BSG/ESGE warns of contraindication below 30 and requires hematology input for rapidly deteriorating renal function; no automatic timing. |
| CrCl 49.5, 79.5, 80, 80.5 | Do not round or interpolate across ASGE table bands; retain raw CrCl and source-specific inclusive/exclusive boundaries. |
| AF vs acute/recent VTE, recent stroke, mechanical valve | Confirm indication, thrombosis risk, recent events, and whether dabigatran is appropriate; do not extrapolate generic AF tables to VTE or mechanical valves. |
| Combined EGD/colonoscopy | Assess highest anticipated intervention risk; possible therapeutic polypectomy requires explicit planning, not default diagnostic-risk classification. |
| Concurrent antiplatelet therapy | Review aspirin/P2Y12 indication, recent stent/ACS, dual/triple therapy; dabigatran table alone does not authorize antiplatelet interruption. |
| Restart and bridging | BSG/ESGE and IDEN restart depends on hemostasis; ACG/CAG does not establish exact same-day restart. Routine heparin bridging of DOAC interruption is not supported by these pathways. |
| Pediatric formulation/enteral administration, significant liver disease, anesthesia | Adult elective-endoscopy timing must not automatically inherit; assess separately. Aspiration/NPO is an anesthesia policy question, not a reason to change the anticoagulant guideline hold interval. |
