## Primary passage verification performed in this execution
- ASGE 2016 official PDF, Table 7: https://www.asge.org/docs/default-source/education/practice_guidelines/doc-antithrombotics.pdf ; DOI 10.1016/j.gie.2015.09.035. Apixaban high-risk endoscopy CrCl >60: 1 or 2 days; 30–59: 3 days; 15–29: 4 days. Exactly 60 and outside the listed brackets are not unambiguously resolved by the printed table.
- BSG/ESGE 2021 official guideline, DOAC section: https://www.esge.com/assets/downloads/pdfs/guidelines/2021_a_1547_2282.pdf ; DOI 10.1136/gutjnl-2021-325184. Low-risk: omit morning DOAC dose (weak/low); high-risk: last DOAC dose 3 days prior (strong/low); restart within 2–3 days depending on bleeding/thrombotic risk (strong/moderate).
- ACG/CAG 2022: https://doi.org/10.14309/ajg.0000000000001627 ; conditional/very-low-certainty suggestion for temporary DOAC interruption in elective endoscopy; no universal apixaban-specific timestamp and no recommendation for same-day vs 1–7-day DOAC resumption.
- CHEST 2022: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chest.2022.07.025 ; conditional, very-low-certainty perioperative apixaban interruption 1–2 days; generally 24h low/moderate-risk vs 48–72h high-risk resumption, subject to hemostasis.
- AHA/ACC 2024: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacc.2024.06.013 ; section 7.6 and Tables 13–14, noncardiac surgery comparator, not an endoscopy-specific mandate.

## Explicit disagreements
ASGE 2016 low-risk continuation vs BSG/ESGE 2021 morning omission vs ACG/CAG 2022 temporary interruption. High-risk ASGE CrCl-specific 1–4 days vs BSG/ESGE last dose 3 days vs CHEST 1–2 days. Keep pathways separate. Do not use FDA labeling as hold-duration authority or require label reconciliation.
