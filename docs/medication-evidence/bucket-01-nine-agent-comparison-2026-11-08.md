**Guideline conflict:** ASGE 2016 often permits continuing DOACs in low-risk endoscopy, whereas ACG/CAG 2022 conditionally suggests interruption and BSG/ESGE 2021 suggests omitting the morning dose. For high-risk endoscopy ASGE renal tables, BSG/ESGE last-dose schedules, and CHEST generic perioperative timing differ. Preserve alternatives for clinician choice; never collapse into a single automated instruction.

**General scenario coverage:** diagnostic EGD/colonoscopy with or without biopsy versus polypectomy/EMR/ESD/ERCP sphincterotomy/EUS sampling; indication and recent VTE/stent/ACS; low/high thrombotic risk; CKD and unstable renal function where relevant; DAPT/triple therapy; last-dose and restart timing; hemostasis and delayed bleeding; clinician/institutional variations. Combination therapy must be assessed separately. No patient-facing activation.

## 1. rivaroxaban — ING-rivaroxaban
Class: Xa inhibitor. Provisional mapping: one canonical ID per Bucket 01 assignment; full formulation inheritance and regression testing still required.
- **ASGE 2016 historical comparator:** ASGE Table 8: CrCl >90 >=1 day; 60–90 2 days; 30–59 3 days; 15–29 4 days. Source: ASGE 2016 tables/recommendations above.
- **BSG/ESGE 2021:** BSG/ESGE: omit morning dose for low risk; last dose 3 days before high-risk. Source: 2021 figures/recommendations above.
- **ACG/CAG 2022:** For DOACs, conditional very-low-certainty suggestion to interrupt temporarily; for warfarin, generally suggests continuation for elective endoscopy and against bridging if held except selected mechanical-valve contexts; for DAPT suggests temporarily interrupt P2Y12 while continuing aspirin; for aspirin secondary prevention suggests continuation. These are conditional/very-low-certainty recommendations as applicable, not universal hold schedules.
- **CHEST/ACC:** Compare indication and procedural risk independently; do not transfer generic surgical schedules to endoscopy without clinician assessment.
- **Outstanding before evidence completion:** confirm exact agent-specific original passage and grade; verify renal/indication/last-dose/resumption scenarios; full mapped-ID and formulation tests; independent verification if requested; executed test and commit evidence. Clinician review remains separate.
