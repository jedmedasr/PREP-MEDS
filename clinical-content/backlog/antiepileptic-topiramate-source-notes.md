## Preserved boundaries

- Immediate-release tablet, sprinkle capsule, oral solution, QUDEXY XR, and TROKENDI XR remain distinct identities; bare topiramate and bare TOPAMAX fail closed.
- Existing QSYMIA phentermine/topiramate remains a whole combination-product identity and is not decomposed.
- Indication, seizure/migraine context, last dose, adherence, renal function, bicarbonate/metabolic-acidosis risk, hydration status, concomitant carbonic-anhydrase inhibitors, oral feasibility, withdrawal risk, and anesthesia plan remain clinician/local-policy questions.
