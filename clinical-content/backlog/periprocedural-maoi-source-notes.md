These sources authenticate medication identity, route, dosage form, MAOI context, and labeled indication context. They do not authenticate patient-specific manufacturer, NDC, strength, package, commercial availability, substitution, route conversion, or peri-procedural timing.

Phenelzine, tranylcypromine, isocarboxazid, and EMSAM are not interchangeable. Bare selegiline must not resolve to EMSAM because oral selegiline/MAO-B-inhibitor identities are outside this cluster. Concomitant serotonergic, sympathomimetic, opioid, dextromethorphan, anesthetic, and vasopressor exposure; dose; dietary context; cardiovascular status; psychiatric risk; discontinuation; and restart remain accountable clinician/local-policy decisions. No universal hold, washout, taper, anesthesia, vasopressor, or restart rule is authorized.
