# Periprocedural chronic benzodiazepine source notes

Status: DRAFT / REVIEW_REQUIRED. This bounded cluster provides medication-reconciliation identities and clinician/anesthesia review routing only.

Direct DailyMed labeling reconciles XANAX immediate-release and XANAX XR (NDA018276/NDA021434), ATIVAN oral and IM/IV injection (NDA017794/NDA018140), KLONOPIN tablet and Teva clonazepam ODT (NDA017533/ANDA077194), Hospira diazepam IM/IV injection (ANDA071583), and RESTORIL capsule (NDA018163). Bare alprazolam, lorazepam, and clonazepam fail closed across release, route, or form ambiguity. Injectable diazepam is intentionally the only diazepam identity in this bounded cluster and does not assert oral-product coverage. Midazolam and acute procedural-sedation ordering are excluded because this is chronic home-medication reconciliation, not an anesthesia-ordering system.

Duration/use pattern, indication, last dose, dependence/tolerance, withdrawal or seizure history, age/frailty, hepatic/renal function, COPD/OSA/respiratory risk, concomitant opioids/gabapentinoids/alcohol/other CNS depressants, sedation/anesthesia plan, and transport require accountable clinician/local-policy review. No autonomous continuation, interruption, taper, dose/route/form/release conversion, procedural sedation plan, transport decision, or restart timing is authorized.
