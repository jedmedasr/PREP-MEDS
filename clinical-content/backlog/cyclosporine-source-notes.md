## Fail-closed boundaries

- Modified and nonmodified oral cyclosporine remain distinct and are not treated as bioequivalent or interchangeable.
- SANDIMMUNE oral capsule and intravenous injection remain route/form distinct.
- NEORAL modified capsule and oral solution remain dosage-form distinct.
- Bare cyclosporine and formulation-unspecified cyclosporine capsule are intentionally ambiguous.
- Transplanted organ or nontransplant indication, time since transplant, rejection history, current multidrug regimen, last dose, concentration monitoring, renal/hepatic function, interactions, infection status, and oral tolerance require transplant-team and clinician review.
- Evidence does not assert a patient-specific product, dose, regimen, manufacturer, NDC, package, current availability, conversion, or substitution.
