Status: DRAFT / REVIEW_REQUIRED  
Retrieved: 2026-10-03

## Inventory decision

A genuine V1 peri-procedural gap exists. FDA warns that serious breathing difficulties may occur with gabapentin or pregabalin in patients with respiratory risk factors, including concomitant CNS depressants, reduced lung function, and advanced age. Product labeling also preserves discontinuation/withdrawal, renal-function, sedation, and seizure-context uncertainties. These are review inputs, not autonomous timing rules.

Immediate-release dosage forms, GRALISE, HORIZANT prodrug, LYRICA forms, and LYRICA CR remain distinct. Bare gabapentin, NEURONTIN, pregabalin, or LYRICA wording is ambiguous. Indication, chronicity, last dose, renal function, seizure history, withdrawal risk, respiratory disease, age/frailty, concomitant opioids/CNS depressants, oral feasibility, sedation plan, and procedure context require accountable review.
