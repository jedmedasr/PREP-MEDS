# Medication Research Backlog

This directory contains the governed medication research queue used by the Stage 6 knowledge pipeline.

`medication-backlog.json` is intentionally a research-priority artifact, not a source of clinical recommendations. Inclusion of a medication does not imply a hold, continue, or escalation instruction.

Status progression is governed by `@prep-meds/knowledge-pipeline`. Automation may research, structure evidence, draft rules, and technically test content, but cannot transition a medication to `VALIDATED` without accountable clinical governance.

The initial Tier 1 list is an architecture-spanning set used to exercise anticoagulation, antiplatelet, diabetes, GLP-1, insulin, cardiovascular, and simple continuation-style pathways. The formulary will be expanded systematically in later medication build-out work.
